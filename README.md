# RollPhase

**Train near you · every sport · your people.**

Find places to train, partners at your level, events worth showing up for, and gear nearby. Rate gyms after you train. Use one sport or many. Personalize how you represent your club.

1. Open the live beta on a phone or narrow browser window  
2. Accept the welcome terms  
3. Explore Home, Gyms, Partners, Feed, Profile  
4. Send **Feedback** from the top-right
